Darken dims the screen with a full-screen overlay when the system brightness is still too high. You can adjust the dimming level from 0 to 99% and choose a gray, warm brown, red, or custom color tint.

Toggle the overlay from the app, a Quick Settings tile, or a home screen widget. Optional notification controls let you adjust dimming while it is active. Settings stay on your device.

Darken requires Android 12 or newer and the Display over other apps permission. It has no ads, analytics, accounts, or Internet permission. If your device provides Android's built-in Extra dim feature, that is the most integrated choice; Darken offers additional tint and control options.

Some security-sensitive apps may block interaction while an overlay is active. Disable the overlay when using those apps.
